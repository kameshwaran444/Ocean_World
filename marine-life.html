Marine Life

The ocean is home to an extraordinary range of living things. From tiny plankton to giant whales, every organism is part of a connected marine ecosystem.

Meet ocean animals

Dolphins

Dolphins are intelligent marine mammals. They live in social groups and use sounds to communicate and find their way underwater.

Reef fish

Reef fish come in many shapes and colours. They find food and shelter among coral structures and other reef habitats.
