The rainforests of the sea

Coral Reefs

Coral reefs are living structures formed by tiny animals called coral polyps. They provide food, shelter and nursery areas for many kinds of marine life.

Rich habitats

Reefs support a wide variety of fish, invertebrates, algae and other organisms. Their complex shapes create many small habitats.

Colourful neighbours

Many reef fish rely on coral habitats for protection and food. Healthy reefs help support healthy ocean ecosystems.

How can we protect coral reefs?

  • Reduce plastic use and keep litter away from waterways.
  • Choose reef-safe behaviour when visiting marine habitats.
  • Do not touch, break or collect coral.
  • Support responsible conservation and ocean education.
